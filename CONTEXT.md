# membrs

A discovery app for African cities: what is on, what is open, what closes
soon and what is on offer, sorted and sent as one email every Sunday. It
starts in Lagos, Abuja and Accra.

## Language

### Product

**membrs**:
The product. Lower case in running text, MEMBRS as the wordmark.
_Avoid_: Members, the platform

**Reader**:
Someone browsing membrs or reading the digest. No account is needed.
_Avoid_: user, member, customer

### What membrs lists

**Listing**:
An **Event** or an **Opportunity**. Places and promos are not listings; they have their own shapes.

**Event**:
Something happening at a time and place, or online. Has a date, time, venue and price.
_Avoid_: happening, activity

**Opportunity**:
Something with a deadline you apply to: a grant, fellowship, job, hackathon, call for speakers.
_Avoid_: gig, offer

**Place**:
Somewhere to go any day: a beach, park, museum, library. Has opening hours and entry.
_Avoid_: venue (a venue is where an event happens)

**Promo**:
A deal from a business, with how to claim it and when it ends.
_Avoid_: discount, coupon, ad

**Category**:
What a listing is about (Music, Grants). Places have a **type** (Beaches, Libraries) instead.

**View**:
A time slice of a section: Tonight, This Weekend, Closing Next Week, Open Now. Views and categories are picked from the same title picker.
_Avoid_: group, bucket

### The digest

**Sunday Digest**:
The weekly email, 7 AM every Sunday, tuned to a city and interests.
_Avoid_: newsletter (in copy), mailing list

**Issue**:
One edition of the digest, also published on the site under `/digest`.

**Interests**:
The categories and types a reader picks for their digest.

### Contributing

**Contribution**:
Anything sent in for review: an event, opportunity, place, promo or an ad request.
_Avoid_: post, upload

**Ad**:
A paid placement. It is reviewed like every other listing.
